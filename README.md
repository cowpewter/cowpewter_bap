# Seed & Stock: Swords to Plowshares

**A cozy, combat-free farming and animal-breeding pack for Minecraft 1.21.1.**

The land has been at peace for as long as anyone can remember. There are no swords. There are no monsters. There is only the calm, peaceful, rural life of the farmer.

![A sunny day at the farm](/docs/img/day_farm.png)

---

## 🧬 Evolution, hand picked by you

[![bap_madefor_badge](https://i.ibb.co/C5gCk6Rw/bap1-madefor-64h.png)](https://discord.gg/r3uTFGfkgf)

*Made for the Big Autumn Packjam 2026, theme: **Evolution**.*

Nature doesn't drive the evolution of your herd - you do. Selectively breed your animals to improve their stats: yield, fertility, growth rate and constitution. Discover unique genetic traits like Gluttonous Pigs or Broody Chickens. Powered by [Animal Husbandry](https://modrinth.com/mod/animalhusbandry).

### New Tools

Use the Magnifying Glass to inspect your livestock's health, and the Farm Ledger to see their genetics.

![The Magnifying Glass lets you see an animal's health](/docs/img/magnifying_glass.png)

![The Farm Ledger lets you see an animal's genetics](/docs/img/farm_ledger.png)

### New Trades

Find new breeding stock in the wild, or sell crops for cash and buy new animals from the Wandering Trader.

![A list of spawn eggs for sale by the Wandering Trader](/docs/img/trader.png)

### Earn Cash for Crops and Animal Products

Your Coin Pouch will automatically collect your earnings and condense stacks of smaller denominations into larger. Use a Crafting Table to break larger coins down into smaller.

![An open Coin Pouch, displaying its contents.](/docs/img/coin_pouch.png)

### Guide Book

Courtesy of [Questlog](https://modrinth.com/mod/questlog)

An in-game Guide Book provides quests to help you get started. While most of the quests are intended as a gentle introduction to the pack's mechanics, a handful are Just-For-Fun challenge goals, like breeding the Ultimate Beast - an animal with all maximum stats!

![Guide Book main page](/docs/img/book_chapters.png)

![A quest displayed in the Guide Book](/docs/img/book_quest.png)

### Cook Meals to Increase Profits

Cook raw ingredients into a meal, from simple snacks to full on feasts, to increase their value in the Shipping Bin.

Make your perfect cozy kitchen, thanks to [Farmer's Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight)

![A small kitchen lit by sunset and firelight](/docs/img/kitchen.png)
### And Much More!

* You will always spawn somewhere green
* Chop trees from the bottom to fell the whole tree at once
* Copper tools, including Shears, Buckets, Name Tags, and Lanterns
* Wool and Concrete stairs and slabs
* More things to make with Bamboo
* Chests/barrels/animals can be picked up and carried, thanks to [Carry On](https://modrinth.com/mod/carry-on)
* Accelerated travel by road, courtesy of [Via Romana](https://modrinth.com/mod/via-romana). Mounts and carried entities come with you when you teleport via sign.
* Drift safely across gaps and off mountaintops with [Gliders](https://modrinth.com/mod/gliders)
* There's room for all your tools with [Sophisticated Backpacks](https://modrinth.com/mod/sophisticated-backpacks)

## 🐞 Found a bug? Have a suggestion?

Report it on the **[Seed & Stock issue tracker](https://github.com/cowpewter/cowpewter_bap/issues/new/choose)**. Crash Assistant should open when the game crashes, allowing you to easily view/copy/upload your logs. Just paste them into the Issue tracker.
